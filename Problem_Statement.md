# **Problem Statement: Placement Management System (PMS)**

The traditional campus recruitment process in many educational institutions is predominantly manual, fragmented, and inefficient. Training and Placement Cells (TPC) often rely on physical documentation and disparate spreadsheets to manage vast amounts of student data, company requirements, and application records. This lack of a centralized system leads to significant administrative overhead, data redundancy, and a high probability of human error during student eligibility verification and shortlisting.

Furthermore, a critical gap exists in real-time communication between students, recruiters, and the administration. Students frequently miss vital job opportunities or remain uninformed about their application status due to the absence of a streamlined notification system. Recruiters, on the other hand, struggle with the manual screening of resumes and the inability to broadcast job updates efficiently to a targeted student pool.

The **Placement Management System (PMS)** addresses these challenges by providing a premium, automated platform that centralizes the recruitment lifecycle. By integrating features such as dynamic student profiles, real-time job feeds, one-click application tracking, and automated administrative dashboards, the system eliminates manual bottlenecks. It ensures transparent, efficient, and data-driven recruitment, bridging the communication gap and enhancing the overall placement experience for all stakeholders.
